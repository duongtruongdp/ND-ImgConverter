===================================================================

Why does macOS say "The application can't be opened"?

macOS Gatekeeper may block apps downloaded from the internet. 
Try these quick fixes:

Method 1 (Recommended):
1. Drag "ND Image Converter" into your /Applications folder.
2. Open Terminal and run:
   sudo xattr -cr /Applications/ND\ Image\ Converter.app
3. Enter your password and open the app.

Method 2:
Right-click the app in Finder -> Select "Open" -> Click "Open" in the dialog.

Method 3:
Go to System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
