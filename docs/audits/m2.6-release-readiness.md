Audit date: 2026-11-01

## Release gates

The workflow no longer installs FFmpeg from Homebrew, Chocolatey or apt. It runs `scripts/prepare-release-runtime.mjs` and `scripts/verify-release-sources.mjs`; empty or incomplete source metadata stops the job before packaging. Downloader assets are also not eligible for release until their per-target source, checksum and license records are completed.

Do not interpret local Rust/frontend test success as clean-machine or installer evidence.
